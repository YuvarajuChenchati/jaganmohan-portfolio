About

Hi, I'm Jaganmohan.

The best wedding films are the ones you still want to watch years later.

Most people call me Jay. I'm a wedding filmmaker, photographer and video editor, and every film on this page was shot and edited by me, start to finish.

I care about the in-between moments as much as the big ones — a parent's glance during the muhurtham, friends losing it on the dance floor, the quiet minute before the bride walks in. I shoot to capture those honestly, then spend the time in the edit shaping them into a story with the right rhythm, colour and music.

Whether you need a complete wedding film, a set of photographs, or an editor for footage you already have, I'm happy to talk it through.

Questions

Before you book

Can I see a full film before deciding?

Yes. Every film in the "Recent wedding films" section is complete and unedited from what the couple received. Watch as much as you like.

Do you cover both photography and video?

Yes. You can book the film, the photographs, or both, and the reels come from the same coverage.

Can you edit footage we already have?

Yes. Send me what you have and I'll suggest how it can be cut into a film or a set of reels: structure, pacing, colour and sound.

When will we get our film and photos?

Reels usually come first, then the full film and photographs. The exact timeline depends on the events and is agreed before you book.

How do I check if my date is free?

Message me on WhatsApp or email with your wedding date, city and the events you're planning. I'll reply with availability and packages.

Get in touch

Let's make your film.

Tell me your wedding date and city, and I'll send over availability and packages. Popular wedding dates fill up early, so it's worth asking soon.
