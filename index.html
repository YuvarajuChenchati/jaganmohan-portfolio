About

Hi, I'm Jaganmohan.

The best wedding films are the ones you still want to watch years later.

Most people call me Jay. I'm a wedding filmmaker, photographer and video editor, and every film on this page was shot and edited by me, start to finish.

I care about the in-between moments as much as the big ones — a parent's glance during the muhurtham, friends losing it on the dance floor, the quiet minute before the bride walks in. I shoot to capture those honestly, then spend the time in the edit to shape them into a story with the right rhythm, colour and music.

Whether you need a complete wedding film, a set of photographs, or an editor for footage you already have, I'm happy to talk it through.
